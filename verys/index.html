How it works

You describe the question. The agent runs the rest.

Sourcing, the ask, the calendar, and the write-up happen before you join the call.

  1. 01

    Describe what you want to learn

    Say the question, who should answer it, and how many calls you want. A short brief is enough.

  2. 02

    The agent finds the right people

    It looks for people who do that work, and brings you profiles that match the question.

  3. 03

    You approve, they get a fee

    You approve each profile. The people you approve are offered a fee, such as $50 for 30 minutes.

  4. 04

    The call, then the write-up

    The call is scheduled. You join and ask the questions. Afterward you get the transcript and the notes.

Why Verys

Less chasing than a cold DM. More specific than a panel.

The slow parts of customer discovery are finding the right person and getting them on a call. That is the work the agent does.

Cold DMs

  • Few people write back.
  • You spend hours chasing replies.
  • The ones who answer may not do the work.

Research panels

  • Respondents are often generic.
  • People learn how to pass the screener.
  • Minimums get expensive fast.

Verys

  • People are matched to your question.
  • You approve every profile.
  • The call is booked for you.
What you get

The call, the transcript, and the notes.

Three things come back from every conversation.

The call

A conversation with someone you approved. You show up and ask the questions.

The full transcript

Every word from the call, so you can go back to what was said and share it with the people building beside you.

A notes summary

The key points and the quotes worth keeping. The short version of what you heard.
