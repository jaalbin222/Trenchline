How it works

You describe the question. The agent does the rest.

Recruiting, scheduling, and the write-up are the work a person at an agency would do. Here, an agent does them.

  1. 01

    Describe your question

    Say what you need to learn, and who does that work. A sentence is enough to start.

  2. 02

    The agent matches people

    It looks for someone who has done the job, at the tier you asked for, and checks they are a fit.

  3. 03

    The call happens

    The agent puts it on the calendar. You join, and you ask the questions.

  4. 04

    Notes and a transcript

    You get the full transcript, plus notes that pull out the patterns and the quotes.

What you get

The call, the transcript, and the notes.

Three things come back from every conversation. Nothing else to chase down.

The call

A conversation with someone who does the work, runs the team, or decides to buy. You ask. They answer from the job.

The full transcript

Every word from the call, so you can go back to what was said and share it with the people building beside you.

A notes summary

Patterns across what you heard, and the quotes that carry them. The short version, with the lines worth keeping.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

A short version of how a project runs.

What kind of people can I talk to?
Three kinds. A practitioner who does the work. A manager who runs that team. A buyer who decides whether to spend. You choose the tier when you send the brief. The agent looks for someone who has done the job, not a spokesperson.
What does a call cover?
How the work gets done. You ask the questions. The agent finds the person and puts the call on the calendar. Afterward you get the transcript and a notes summary with the patterns and quotes.
Is the call confidential?
The conversation is about the kind of work, not secrets. People are asked not to share private company information, customer names, or plans that are not public. You should not ask for those either.
What if there is no fit?
We tell you. We do not invent a match. You can change the question or the tier, and the agent looks again.
