Trust

Talk about the work, never the employer.

Calls should help a founder and stay safe for the person who takes them.

How, not who

Talk about steps, tools, and what slows the work down. Not customer names, prices, or private plans.

People who do the job

Someone who does this work now, or did it recently, at a company like the one you are learning about.

Clear ground rules

Call-takers agree not to share secrets. Founders agree not to ask for them.

Private by default

No recording unless both people say yes. A first name is enough.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Open the app and ask it there.

Why talk to people in the work?
They are closest to the day-to-day job, the part your product has to fit. They can tell you what really happens.
Can they tell me who buys and what they pay?
Usually not. Use the call to understand the work and the pain. For budget and who decides, also talk to a buyer.
How fast is a call?
You ask a question, we find people who do that work, and you book a 30-minute call. If we cannot find a fit, we say so.
How does payment work?
Founders pay $50 for each 30-minute call. Call-takers receive $35 when the call happens. If no one joins, we rebook or refund you.
Can I take a call if I am in the job now?
Yes, if you talk about how the work is done in general. Do not share secrets about your employer, its customers, or its plans. Check your own agreement. Someone who left, or who does the same job at another company, is a good fit too.
How is this different from running a research study?
A study is built for a research team. Trenchline is for a pre-seed or seed founder who needs a few honest calls with people who do the work.
